    Assumptions in this model
    • One flat federal rate. No state tax, net investment income tax, or payroll tax.
    • A month is 30 days, not a calendar month. The month count is never negative.
    • The growth charts move today’s price by a percentage and apply the same net formula. They do not include dividends, and the result is not discounted.
    • RSUs: the net figure is price × shares × (1 − federal rate). Tax on the next vest is price × vesting shares × federal rate.
    • NSOs: tax on the next vest is that tranche’s bargain element × federal rate. The headline net is the full grant’s bargain element × (1 − federal rate), plus strike × all shares.
    • ISOs: tax on the next vest is that tranche’s bargain element × the AMT rate. The headline net is that tranche’s bargain element minus the AMT estimate, plus strike × all shares. The growth chart uses the NSO formula instead, at the federal rate.
    • Concentration for RSUs uses price × shares. For options it uses the bargain element plus strike × shares.
    • The post-vesting percentage adds the next tranche’s pretax value on top of that position value.
    • The sell and hold lines use those original formulas. Neither line is a suggestion to sell or to hold.
